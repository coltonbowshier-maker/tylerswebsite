00 A different kind of flip

Most renovations chase speed. We chase the sight line, the section, the way morning light lands on white oak.

Every house we take on is treated as a commission — fully designed before the first wall opens, specified to the last cabinet pull, and built by trades we trust. The result feels inevitable. The return follows.

02 How we work

Four moves,
made well.

  1. 01

    Acquire

    Discipline at the buy. We underwrite conservatively and choose houses for their bones — proportion, structure, lot, and light that others walk past.

  2. 02

    Design

    Every project is fully designed before demolition: plans, palettes, millwork, fixtures — specified to the last cabinet pull. Nothing decided on the fly.

  3. 03

    Build

    A short bench of trusted trades, held to furniture-grade tolerances and paid on time. The schedule is honest and the standard doesn't move.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Styled, photographed, and offered as a finished thought — homes that earn their price on the first walk-through, not the second price cut.
