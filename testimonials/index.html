What we're after

Specific beats flattering.

One or two things that stuck with you say more than a paragraph of praise: something he caught that others missed, how the process actually went, whether the finished house lived up to it.

A sentence on how long you've known him or what you worked on together helps too.

For example “I've worked with Tyler for the past four years, and his attention to detail is impeccable. He sees things in a house that no one else does, and every project comes back better than we pictured it.”
