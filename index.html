How we work

Start to finish.

  1. 01

    Acquire

    We buy carefully and keep our numbers conservative. Mostly we’re after good bones: proportion, structure, a decent lot, and light that other buyers walk right past.

  2. 02

    Design

    The whole house is designed before demolition starts. Floor plans, finishes, cabinetry, fixtures. It’s all decided up front, so we’re not making it up as we go.

  3. 03

    Build

    A small crew of trades we trust and pay on time. We hold a high bar on the finish work and keep an honest schedule.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    We style it, photograph it, and list it finished. The goal is a house that sells on the first walk-through, not after a price cut.
